# Sidebar arms: accounting totals (#832)

Companion to [latency/workload tables](stream-sidebar-arms-tables.md) and
[controlled results](stream-sidebar-arms-measured.md). Same groups and all windows.
Each SQL, transaction-statement (Tx), count-statement (C), sent recount (S),
empty-acquire (A), cancelled-acquire (X) and pool-wait (W, seconds) cell is a **sum**
over windows, from the sidebar line. S counts sent snapshots, not worker
computations. W sums empty-acquire time across concurrent callers and both
distinct pools once; it is not wall time. Leases shows median (min–max) of
the aggregate concurrent peak, never a sum of independent pool peaks.
Elapsed shows median (min–max) posting-plus-drain seconds; rate is the logged
sent recounts/stream/s median (min–max). Fixture/admin leases and initial
recounts are excluded. L/R/F and aggregation follow the latency tables.
