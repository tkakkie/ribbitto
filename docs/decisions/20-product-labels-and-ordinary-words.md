# 20. Product labels and ordinary words

**Decided:** the [vocabulary](../domain/vocabulary.md) reserves frog-themed
UI labels; identifiers stay neutral. Everything else uses ordinary words,
localised normally, including identity, roles, visibility, permissions,
notifications, everyday operations and anything people say aloud as an
action ("DM me", "mention her", "react to it"). Product labels use the same
Latin-script words in Japanese as in English. Planned labels do not decide
the conversation model (#266).
**Why:** labels work as names; misread operational words cause permission
or notification mistakes, and words awkward to say go unused.
**Considered:** translating product words; *peep* for direct conversations
(reads as "spy on", wrong for a private place); *duet* ("duet me" is awkward);
*croak* for mentions (slang for "die", and mentions must be understood);
*hop* for reactions (people say "react").
